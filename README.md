## 📌 Project Overview

This project is completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task is to analyze business sales data and identify important patterns related to revenue, products, categories, regions, and profitability.

The analysis was performed using the **Superstore Sales Dataset** and visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Find top-performing products
- Analyze sales across different categories
- Compare sales performance by region
- Analyze profit performance across sub-categories
- Identify areas where profitability can be improved
- Present insights through a client-ready dashboard

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

- **Technology** generated the highest sales among the three main categories.
- **West** was the highest-performing region in terms of sales.
- **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the top product by sales.
- **Copiers** generated the highest profit among the sub-categories.
- **Tables** recorded a negative profit, indicating a potential area for profitability improvement.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings:

1. Focus on high-performing **Technology** products and categories.
2. Study the strategies contributing to strong sales in the **West region**.
3. Promote top-performing products to maximize revenue.
4. Investigate loss-making sub-categories such as **Tables**.
5. Monitor product-level profitability before increasing discounts or promotions.
